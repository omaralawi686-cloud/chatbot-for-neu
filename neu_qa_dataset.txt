Q: What is Near East University?
A: Near East University (NEU), known in Turkish as Yakın Doğu Üniversitesi (YDÜ), is a private university founded in 1988 in North Nicosia, Northern Cyprus. It is the largest university in Northern Cyprus, with over 25,000 students from more than 100 countries, 20 faculties, and 98+ departments.
